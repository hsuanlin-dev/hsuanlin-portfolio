## Overview

I was the sole person responsible for the visual layer of the Indigenous Carbon Initiative, an ongoing ESG initiative that connects corporate carbon-credit goals with land conservation efforts in Indigenous communities.
National Taipei University served as the primary bridge between the platform and more than 20 participating companies. During the early stages of the project, the website was also presented publicly at the Legislative Yuan.

## Challenge

The platform needed to remain fast and reliable in remote mountainous areas where internet connectivity can be unstable and bandwidth is limited.
At the same time, I had to consolidate feedback from more than 20 participating companies into a cohesive design direction. From visual identity and brand design to website content and copywriting, I was solely responsible for the entire process, with no dedicated design or copywriting team to share the workload.

## What I Did

- Built the initial version of the platform, then iterated on the design based on feedback and discussions with more than 20 participating companies.
- Designed the visual identity and core brand symbols from scratch, while also writing the website's narrative content and copy.
- Built the frontend with Bootstrap and vanilla JavaScript instead of a heavier modern framework, keeping the site fast and reliable under the limited network conditions common in remote communities.
- Developed a custom SCSS architecture, defining the color system and responsive layouts.

## Outcome

Refined through feedback from participating companies, the platform was publicly presented at the Legislative Yuan during the early stages of the project and continues to serve as a promotional platform for National Taipei University's ESG carbon-credit partnership initiative with Indigenous communities.
